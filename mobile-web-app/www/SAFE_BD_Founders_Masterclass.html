How GPS Pings Work (Satellites vs. Towers vs. Wi-Fi)

A phone uses a hybrid positioning technique called A-GPS (Assisted GPS) combining three physical layers:

1. US NAVSTAR Satellites
The phone listens to atomic clocks from 4+ satellites orbiting 20,000 km in space. Calculates outdoor location to sub-5 meter accuracy.
2. Cellular Triangulation
Measures signal delay between 3 neighboring mobile towers (GP, Robi, Teletalk). Provides instant rough location in under 1 second while satellites lock.
3. Wi-Fi BSSID Mapping
Detects nearby router signals indoors to calculate accurate floor and building position even without direct sky visibility.

The 4 Shields That Make SAFE bd Unhackable

Governments care intensely about data security, privacy, and sovereignty. Here are the 4 cryptographic pillars running inside your platform:

1. Encryption in Transit (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS / WSS)
Every coordinate, password, and video stream is wrapped in military-grade AES-256-GCM encryption before leaving the phone. Anyone attempting to sniff Wi-Fi traffic at a café or mobile hotspot sees only scrambled mathematical noise.
2. Zero-Knowledge Password Hashing (PBKDF2)
Passwords are never saved in plaintext. We run them through PBKDF2 with 100,000 computational rounds plus a unique 16-byte random salt. Even if an attacker physically stole the database, reversing the passwords is mathematically impossible.
3. IDOR Defense (Insecure Direct Object Reference)
Prevents hackers from changing a URL parameter to steal another citizen's data. In SAFE bd, the server validates the cryptographic Bearer token on every request: User A can only query User A's contacts.
4. Immutable Chain of Custody for Evidence
When covert video uploads, an SHA-256 cryptographic hash and ISO server timestamp are locked with it. If an attacker later claims "that video was edited or AI-generated", the cryptographic hash proves in court that the footage is authentic and untouched.

Q2: "What happens if a victim has zero mobile data balance or 4G is jammed?"
Your Winning Answer: "We built dual-redundancy. When high-speed data is available, it streams video and WebSockets. Q4: "How do you prevent pranksters or malicious actors from spamming fake SOS calls?"
