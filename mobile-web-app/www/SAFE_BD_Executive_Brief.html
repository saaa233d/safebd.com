1. Operational Problem: The Distress Response Gap

When a citizen faces imminent violence, harassment, assault, abduction, or road trauma, conventional voice telephony (calling 999) poses critical real-world limitations: victims often cannot speak aloud without provoking attackers, cannot accurately describe ambiguous landmarks in darkness, and frequently have their phones snatched or destroyed before emergency responders receive dispatchable telemetry.

SAFE bd was engineered to eliminate this response gap through an autonomous, silent, one-touch emergency protocol that broadcasts continuous GPS coordinates, auto-captures tamper-proof video/audio evidence, and maintains a proactive virtual escort for citizens navigating vulnerable routes.

2. Functional Core: Systems Developed & Verified
A. Instant Silent SOS & Telemetry Burst Life-Critical
Triggered via a single button tap or hardware lock-screen shortcut without needing to unlock the phone or speak:
  • Zero-Latency Coordinates: Transmits high-accuracy latitude/longitude, speed, and heading to designated emergency contacts and dispatch.
  • Telemetry Handshake: Automatically packages device battery percentage, network connection quality, and exact ISO timestamp.
  • Multi-Channel Dispatch: Initiates simultaneous WebSocket alarm streams, automated SMS fallbacks, and prioritized direct 999 fast-dialing.
B. 10-Second Covert Evidence Vault Forensic-Grade
Solves the common law enforcement challenge of destroyed or stolen victim devices:
  • Silent Camera Capture: Instantly activates front/rear sensors to record audio and video without flashing or displaying preview UI.
  • Cloud Streaming & Immutable Lock: Encrypts and transmits the media file directly to a cloud evidence vault within seconds.
  • Legal Chain of Custody: Even if the attacker snatches the phone and smashes it, the recorded incident evidence is permanently preserved for prosecution.
C. "Walk With Me" Virtual Safety Escort Transit Security
Designed specifically for women, evening commuters, and students traversing unlit or isolated transit corridors:
  • Continuous Safety Heartbeat: The citizen enters an estimated time of arrival (ETA). A live timer monitors transit progress.
  • Dead-Man's Switch Logic: If the citizen does not enter their private PIN upon arrival or deviates off-route, the system automatically triggers an escalating emergency distress alert.
  • Discreet Decoy Utility: Includes a realistic simulated incoming call screen allowing citizens to gracefully de-escalate uncomfortable encounters.
D. Triple-Shield Emergency Circle Zero-Loss Sync
Guarantees trusted family and guardians are reachable during severe network interruptions:
  • Tri-Tier Contact Registry: Dedicated pre-configured slots for Father, Mother, and Legal Guardian with direct carrier dial triggers.
  • Universal Device Persistence: Saved emergency numbers reside in tamper-proof hardware storage, immune to session logouts or server restarts.
  • Cross-Cloud Auto-Healing: Replicates contact records across encrypted database tables and cloud storage with instant fallback.

4. Geo-Awareness & Verified Community Reporting
Interactive GIS Hazard & Safety Map Spatial Intel

Visualizes real-time civic safety layers across metropolitan areas. Citizens can query verified police stations (Thanas), emergency trauma centers, public safe-zones, poorly illuminated streets, and recently reported civic disturbances within a 5-kilometer radius.

Anti-Rumor Moderation Pipeline Verification Filter

To prevent public panic, misinformation, or malicious propaganda, all citizen incident submissions pass through an administrative gatekeeping queue. Only reports confirmed with authentic timestamps, geo-coordinates, and verified facts are approved for public broadcast.

5. Dispatch Integration: 999 CAD Command Portal

SAFE bd is not merely a citizen application; it includes a dedicated Law Enforcement & Emergency Dispatcher Console engineered to integrate seamlessly alongside the Bangladesh Police 999 Computer-Aided Dispatch (CAD) infrastructure:

Real-Time Emergency Telemetry Pipeline:
Citizen SOS Trigger ──► Secure WSS Gateway ──► 999 CAD Dispatch Map ──► Cloud Video Vault ──► Nearest Patrol Unit
Live Dispatcher Map Operations

Displays incoming alarms with blinking red priority indicators, caller phone, verified national identity, and live breadcrumb vector updates.

Instant Media Player Forensics

Enables 999 call-takers to immediately view or listen to the 10-second covert video clip uploaded from the incident scene for situational assessment.

Patrol Vectoring Rapid Response

Generates instant navigation routes for the closest police patrol vehicle, reducing arrival time during life-threatening emergencies.
