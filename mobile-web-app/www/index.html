Critical First Aid Protocols Immediate step-by-step actions before medics arrive
Severe Bleeding & Trauma
Direct pressure, elevation & clotting
1
Apply Firm Direct Pressure: Place a clean cloth, sterile gauze, or clothing over the wound and press firmly with both hands without lifting.
2
Elevate the Injured Limb: Raise the bleeding area above heart level if no bone fracture is suspected to reduce blood flow.
3
Do Not Remove Soaked Dressing: If blood seeps through, place another cloth directly on top of it. Removing the first cloth disrupts clotting.
Caution: Never pull out embedded knives, glass, or foreign objects. Secure them in place with rolled cloths and call 999.
Hands-Only CPR & Choking
Cardiac arrest & Heimlich maneuver
1
Check Response & Call 999: Shake shoulders gently and shout. If no normal breathing or response, call 999 CAD immediately.
2
Hard & Fast Chest Compressions: Place heel of hand on center of chest. Interlock fingers. Push hard and fast (100–120 bpm, ~2 inches deep).
3
For Choking (Heimlich Maneuver): Stand behind victim. Give 5 sharp blows between shoulder blades, then 5 quick inward & upward abdominal thrusts above the navel.
Burns & Chemical Scalds
Cool tap water & sterile protection
1
Cool Under Running Water for 20 Mins: Immediately flush the burn under cool, gentle tap water. This halts the thermal burn process in deep tissue.
2
Cover Loosely: Use sterile non-stick plastic wrap (cling film) or clean lint-free cloth to shield from airborne infections.
3
Remove Accessories: Quickly and gently remove rings, watches, or tight clothing around the burn before swelling starts.
Never: Apply ice, toothpaste, butter, raw egg, or oil. Never pop blisters or peel clothing stuck to burnt skin.
Snake Bite Emergency Protocol
Immobilization & anti-venom hospital transfer
1
Keep Patient Absolutely Calm: Panic increases heart rate, circulating venom faster. Lie down and keep the bitten limb still below heart level.
2
Immobilize with a Splint: Tie a soft splint or bandage comfortably around the limb (firm but NOT a tight tourniquet) to prevent movement.
3
Rush to Government Hospital: Immediately transfer to the nearest Upazila Health Complex, District Sadar Hospital, or Medical College Hospital with Anti-Venom.
Dangerous Myths: DO NOT cut the bite mark, DO NOT suck venom, DO NOT apply battery acid/herbal paste, and DO NOT apply tight arterial tourniquets.
