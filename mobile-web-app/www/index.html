Life-Saving First Aid Protocols Verified emergency actions before ambulance arrival
Severe Bleeding & Deep Wounds
1
Apply Firm Direct Pressure: Place a clean cloth, sterile pad, or clothing directly over the wound and press down hard with both hands.
2
Do Not Remove Blood-Soaked Cloth: Add more pads directly on top. Removing the original pad tears away blood clots and accelerates bleeding.
3
Elevate Above Heart: If an extremity is bleeding, keep the limb elevated above heart level while pressing firmly.
Never Do This: Do not wash deep arterial wounds with water. Do not remove penetrating objects (knives, glass shards) as they plug the hemorrhage.
Choking / Airway Obstruction
1
5 Firm Back Blows: Stand behind the person, bend them forward at the waist, and deliver 5 firm heel-of-hand blows between shoulder blades.
2
5 Abdominal Thrusts (Heimlich): Wrap arms around waist, place a fist just above the navel, and pull sharply upward and inward.
3
If Victim Collapses Unconscious: Lower them gently to the floor and immediately begin CPR chest compressions.
Never Do This: Never perform blind finger sweeps in the throat as this lodges the foreign object deeper.
Burns & Thermal Scalds
1
Cool with Tap Water for 20 Minutes: Hold under clean, room-temperature running water. This halts internal thermal tissue destruction.
2
Remove Jewelry & Tight Items: Swelling begins immediately; remove rings, watches, and loose clothing before constriction occurs.
3
Cover Loosely with Clean Film or Sterile Gauze: Protect raw dermis from airborne infection without sticking to the wound.
Dangerous Myths: NEVER apply ice, toothpaste, butter, egg whites, or mud to burns. Do not pop blisters.
Snake Bite Emergency
1
Keep Patient Absolutely Calm & Still: Movement spreads venom rapidly through the lymphatic system. Keep limb below heart level.
2
Immobilize with a Soft Splint: Tie a soft splint or broad bandage comfortably around the limb to halt muscle pumping (NOT a tourniquet).
3
Rush to Government Anti-Venom Hospital: Immediately transfer to the nearest Upazila Health Complex or District Hospital with anti-venom.
Dangerous Myths: DO NOT cut the bite wound, DO NOT suck venom, DO NOT apply battery acid, herbal paste, or tight arterial tourniquets.
