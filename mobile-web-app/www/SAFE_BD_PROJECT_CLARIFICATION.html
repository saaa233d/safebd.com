1. Executive Summary & Problem Statement Crisis Response Architecture

The Critical Safety Dilemma in Bangladesh: During sudden street attacks, harassment, kidnappings, or highway accidents, victims face three fatal hurdles: (1) unlocking a phone and verbally communicating coordinates is impossible under duress, (2) stolen or destroyed smartphones permanently destroy all video/audio evidence, and (3) finding local thana OCs, fire units, or disaster lines during panic is chaotic.

What Saad Rizwan Built: A full-stack, rapid-dispatch platform engineered to turn any smartphone into a life-saving defense terminal. With a single covert gesture, SAFE bd locks in live GPS coordinates, streams 15s encrypted video/audio to Google Drive, dispatches to 999 CAD, alerts family via WhatsApp, and provides 24/7 localized emergency information via shurokhaAI.

2. Core Functional Systems Engineered Zero-Regression Verified
🚨 One-Touch SOS & 5s Abort Window
Deliberate 3-second haptic hold on the central SOS disc prevents false alarms. A visual 5-second countdown with immediate cancel option enables users to abort accidental triggers before automated dispatch.
🎥 15s Covert Video & Google Drive Vault
Silently records 15 seconds of high-resolution video and ambient audio in the background. Streams directly to backend transcoding and automatically archives to a permanent Google Drive Cloud Vault even if the phone is destroyed.
🗺️ Admin Command Panel with Live Coordinates
Law enforcement and system administrators receive real-time incident notifications showing the victim's verified name, phone number, exact latitude/longitude, and an interactive Google Maps pinpoint for rapid dispatch.
📞 National 999 CAD Direct Dialing
Direct native dialer link (tel:999) triggers instant cellular calling to Bangladesh's National 999 Emergency Dispatch with zero pop-up delays, connecting callers to police, fire, and ambulance operators.
👨‍👩‍👧 Trusted Family Circle (01XXXXXXXXX)
Supports Bangladeshi mobile operators (013-019). Unconfigured contacts display as 01XXXXXXXXX. Users tap the pencil icon to update Father, Mother, or Guardian numbers, securely saved to their verified profile database.
🚶 "Walk With Me" Transit Navigation
Dedicated companion for late-night commuters. Features Bangladesh-wide place autocomplete, Leaflet/Google vector mapping, active route tracking, and automated check-in dead-man timers for high-risk journeys.
AI shurokhaAI — 24/7 National Emergency Conversational Assistant (Active & Functional)
Built directly into the bottom-left of the SOS screen. Backed by real-time Google search grounding and specialized safety rules for Bangladesh. Instantly provides official phone numbers for police thana OCs across all 64 districts, Fire Service hotlines (16163), and Climate/Disaster control (1090). Built strictly without audio voice output to prevent acoustic leakage during concealment, and engineered with intelligent z-index layering (z-index: 25 beneath modals at z-index: 2500) to guarantee zero UI obstruction.

4. Security, Privacy & Zero-Leak Protocol Production Isolation
🔒 Zero-Secret Version Control Isolation

All sensitive files (.env, private databases, user tokens, Google service account keys) are quarantined on the host server. The automated build pipeline (prep_folder.ps1) asserts zero secret leakage before code is pushed to GitHub.

🛡️ Consent-Driven Covert Capture

Hardware sensors (camera, microphone, and geolocation) are only accessed when the citizen actively presses SOS or starts a Walk session. Streams are strictly transmitted over encrypted HTTPS to secure cloud vaults.
