🛡️ Executive Mission & The Critical Problem Solved
When life-threatening crises occur across Bangladesh—street harassment, highway accidents, violent muggings, or medical emergencies—citizens lose critical time: (1) opening a phone and typing coordinates under panic takes minutes victims do not have, (2) snatched or smashed devices destroy all video/audio proof, and (3) localized thana, fire, or disaster hotlines are scattered and inaccessible. What Saad Rizwan Engineered: A unified, military-grade civic safety terminal that instantly locks satellite GPS, streams 15s encrypted covert video to a Google Drive Cloud Vault, alerts 999 CAD, broadcasts live WhatsApp coordinates to family, and delivers instant 64-district emergency data via shurokhaAI.

The 6 Core Safety Subsystems Built
Zero-Regression Verified
🚨 One-Touch SOS Disc (Hold 3s)
Haptic-responsive central disc requiring a deliberate 3-second press to prevent accidental triggers. Displays a 5-second countdown abort window before automated dispatch.
🎥 15s Covert Video & Google Drive Vault
Uses background MediaRecorder APIs to capture 15s of video and audio silently. Automatically transcodes and archives to Google Drive even if the phone is snatched.
🗺️ Admin Live Incident GPS Console
Authorized control room receives instant distress alerts showing the citizen's verified identity, phone number, exact latitude/longitude, and a live Google Maps pin.
📞 National 999 Direct CAD Calling
Direct integration via native dialer protocol (tel:999) connects callers directly to Bangladesh National Emergency Police, Fire, and Ambulance CAD operators.
👨‍👩‍👧 Family Circle & WhatsApp Live GPS
Supports Bangladeshi operators (013-019), showing 01XXXXXXXXX placeholder until edited. Features pencil modal updates and one-tap WhatsApp live GPS dispatch.
🚶 "Walk With Me" Transit Navigation
Interactive navigation with 64-district autocomplete search, Leaflet/Google vector mapping, route tracing, and automated check-in dead-man timers for late-night transit.
AI
shurokhaAI — 24/7 National Emergency Conversational Directory
Active • Grounded in Google Search
Integrated into the bottom-left of the SOS page. Tailored specifically for Bangladesh: uses real-time Google search grounding to provide verified phone numbers for police thana OCs across all 64 districts, Fire Service hotlines (16163), and Climate/Disaster control (1090). Built strictly text-only to avoid acoustic voice leakage during hiding or concealment, and engineered with strict z-index layering (z-index: 25 beneath modals at z-index: 2500) to ensure zero UI obstruction.
