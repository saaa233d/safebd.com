The core SOS subsystem provides immediate, high-priority lifelines when a citizen is threatened, cornered, or experiencing medical trauma:

🚨 Instant One-Tap & Triple-Tap SOS Trigger Critical Lifeline

📞 Automated 999 National Emergency CAD Dispatch Integration Official Protocol
Direct bridge to Bangladesh’s National Emergency Hotline 999.
  • Instant 1-Click Dial: Initiates direct tel-protocol connection to 999 dispatchers with caller identification and auto-prepared case notes.
  • WebSocket CAD Dispatcher: Concurrently broadcasts emergency payload to law enforcement dispatch consoles with live location updates.
📹 Covert Video & Audio Evidence Recording Vault Anti-Tamper
Autonomous background evidence capture during emergencies.
  • Background Media Capture: Records live ambient audio and camera clips covertly, preventing attackers from noticing or deleting recordings.
  • Direct 5TB Vault Upload: Recordings bypass device storage and upload directly to SAFE bd's secure Google Drive Vault and Supabase cloud. Even if the citizen’s phone is smashed or stolen, the evidence remains intact for police prosecution.

3. "Walk With Me" Personal Companion Guard (Commuter Safety)

Engineered for women, students, and late-night commuters traveling through high-risk urban or rural corridors:

🚗 Vehicle Registration & Dhaka Metro Plate Tracking Commuter Shield
Prior to boarding public transit, rideshares, or rickshaws, commuters enter the vehicle's identity.
  • Bangladesh Plate Standard: Pre-configured for official BRTA classifications (e.g. ঢাকা মেট্রো, চট্টগ্রাম মেট্রো, গ, ঘ, থ, ছ, etc.) + 6-digit registration numbers.
  • Walking Mode: One-tap toggle for on-foot travel where no vehicle plate is required.
  • Floating Apple-Style Journey Dock: Real-time journey bar displays remaining time, travel mode, and registered plate number throughout transit.
⏱️ Journey Safety Timer & Proximity Geofencing (10-15m Auto-Arrival) Intelligent Automation
Autonomous journey monitoring using satellite GPS coordinates.
  • Custom Safety Durations: Commuters set predicted travel times (15 min, 30 min, 1 hour, or custom).
  • Auto-Stop Geofence: Automatically detects when the user arrives within 10–15 meters of their designated destination, automatically disarming the safety timer and sending an arrival confirmation.
  • Automated Overdue Escalation: If the timer expires without user confirmation, the system triggers the "Did you reach home safely?" safety modal and prepares automatic distress escalation.
💬 Direct WhatsApp Emergency Distress Dispatch Instant Family Alert
Replaces slow SMS with instant, rich-text WhatsApp emergency dispatches.
  • 1-Tap WhatsApp SOS: Clicking emergency opens WhatsApp directly to selected guardians with formatted markdown (bold alert, timestamp, address).
  • Auto-Attached Live GPS Link: Includes a direct Google Maps URL (https://maps.google.com/?q=lat,lon) showing exact coordinates.
  • Auto-Attached Vehicle Plate: Automatically includes the car/CNG number so family and police know exactly which vehicle to intercept.
  • Universal Share-All: Broadcasts the distress beacon to multiple WhatsApp contacts or family groups in one click.
